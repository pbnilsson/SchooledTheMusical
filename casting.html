The concept

Like Hamilton meets The Sound of Music meets Good Night and Good Luck — all set in and around a high school in the present day — Schooled is about three friends as they move from their school years into their late twenties amidst the culture wars. Both buoyant and unflinching, it examines how today’s turbulent times affect who they are, who they dream to be, and what they can and cannot learn in school.

Schooled’s two acts are set ten years apart: Act I in adolescence, and Act II as they seek to establish careers as a journalist, teacher, and candidate, their youthful experiences ricocheting into their adult, professional lives.

Musical style

A fusion of styles. The score mixes hip-hop, pop, Latin, classical, jazz, and spirituals.

Actors must be able to navigate contemporary musical theater styles as well as more classical influences. Every role must be able to handle complex rhythm and flow, blending rhythmic dialogue and rap with musical theater.
